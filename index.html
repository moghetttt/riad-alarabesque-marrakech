An Oasis of Calm in Marrakesh

أهلاً وسهلاً بكم

Situated in a historic residential alley in the Medina, Riad L'Arabesque offers a tranquil escape just a 6-minute walk from the iconic Jemaa el-Fnaa square and 15 minutes from the 12th-century Koutoubia Mosque.

Feast your eyes on handcrafted zellige tiles, carved cedar wood, and soothing fountain sounds. Every corner balances authentic heritage architecture with serene comfort.
